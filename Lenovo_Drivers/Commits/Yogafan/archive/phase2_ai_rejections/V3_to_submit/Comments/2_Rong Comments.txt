21Q4 (ThinkBook 14 G7+ ASP) is only sold in China and hence missing from
PSREF. Its 16-inch variant (21Q5, ThinkBook 16 G7+ ASP, which shares the
same BIOS) is sold worldwide though.

Its RPM multiplier is 100. The minimum and maximum RPM from
LENOVO_FAN_TEST_DATA are 2200 and 5000, though the fan stops when the
CPU is cool. Data from \_SB.PCI0.LPC0.EC0.FA1S is always the up-to-date
measured RPM, instead of RPM target. It can report RPMs lower or higher
than the minimum or maximum, as the fan can spin slow or faster than the
minimum or maximum too. When the reported RPM is 0, the fan stops.

You may also want to check for more information about the fan control on
my device here:
https://lore.kernel.org/all/d498a1ca58eac5689dae68fffc29440ba75a5faf.camel@rong.moe/
